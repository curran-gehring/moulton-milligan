## φωτισμός,

like its verb (*q.v.*), is applied to spiritual illumination in 2 Cor 4⁴˒⁶, and the corr. use of both verb and subst. in the OT (Pss 18(19)⁸, 26(27)¹) makes it unnecessary with Reitzenstein (*Hellen. Mysterien Religionen*² p. 142 ff.) to look for an explanation of the metaphor in Hellenistic Mystery-Religion. See further Kennedy *St. Paul and the Mystery Religions*, p. 197 f. For the application to "baptism" in ecclesiastical usage, see Justin M. *Apol.* 61E ὁ φωτιζόμενος λούεται, 65C εὐχὰς ποιησάμενοι ὑπὲρ τοῦ φωτισθέντος.
