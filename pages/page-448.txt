μοσχοποιέω.

This compound verb, which in Ac 741 takes the place of
ἐποίησε μόσχον in Ex 324, is claimed by Blass (ad Ac /.c.)
as an example of the faculty which the Greek language never
lost of forming new words. No other instance of it occurs
in the LXX or in profane writers, but it follows the model
of the Platonic εἰδωλοποιέω (Rep. 605 C); cf. εἰδοποιέω
(Plut. Alex. 1), ἀγαλματοποιέω, εἰκονοποιέω.
