## συναναμίγνυμι,

In connexion with the use of this verb in Rom 23⁷⁶ = ''rest along with,'' ''am refreshed in spirit with,'' it is worth recalling that in Eus. *H.E.* iv. 23. 2 Πεμψίμενος is quoted as saying that he spent several days with the Corinthians, during which συνανεκράσημεν τῇ ἑνῇ λόγῳ, ''we were mutually refreshed in the true doctrine.''
