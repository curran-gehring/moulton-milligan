## ἱκμάς.

For the medical usage of this word, which in the NT is confined to Lk 8⁶, see Hobart p. 57 f., but contrast Cadbury *Diction*, p. 43, where it is shown that the word occurs in LXX, Joseph, Plut., Luc., and is, therefore, in no way the sole property of medical writers.
