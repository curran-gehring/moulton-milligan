## Μωϋσῆς.

For this the older form of the proper name, as in the LXX, see Thackeray *Gr.* i. p. 163. The spelling **Μουσῆς, Μουσῆ**, is found in *P Oxy* VIII. 1116²¹ (A.D. 363), *P Grenf* II. 102¹ (vii/A.D.), *al.* See further Preisigke *Namenbuch, s.vv.*
