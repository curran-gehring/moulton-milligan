## ἐπίστασις.

P Amh II. 134⁵ (early ii/A.D.) ἐρω (l. ἐρωτῶ) οὖν σὲ πρὸς
ἐπίστασιν τῶν ἄλλων ἐκδικῆσαι αὐτόν, "I ask you therefore,
in order to check the other elders (?), to vindicate him"
(Edd.), similarly P Oxy XII. 1465¹⁶ (i/B.C.). For the
meaning "attention," which some commentators find in
2 Cor 11²⁸, we may add to the Polybian passages referred to
by Grimm-Thayer, *Aristeas* 256 ἵνα δ' ἐπίστασιν τούτων
λαμβάνωμεν, θεραπεύειν δεῖ τὸν θεόν, "but we must pray to
God for the gift of a regard for these things" (Thackeray)
But both in 2 Cor *l.c.* and Ac 24¹² (cf. 2 Macc 6⁶) the
word is best understood = "onset," or "caballing (con-
spiring) against" (Souter). For the obscure phrase ἐν
ἐπιστάσει καὶ ἐν ἀπολογισμῷ applied to land, see P Tebt I.
p. 576 ff.
